# 動画生成プロンプトサンプル

## 1. 目的

動画生成で使用する prompt と negative prompt の設定例を、今後のプロンプ入力機能の参考用サンプルとして記録する。

記録日: 2026-09-19

## 2. Prompt サンプル

### 2.1 ジャンプ

```text
a girl jumps in place,
looking forward
masterpiece,amazing quality,best quality,detailed hair,detailed eyes
```

### 2.2 回転

```text
a girl turns and spins in place,
masterpiece,amazing quality,best quality,detailed hair,detailed eyes
```

### 2.3 歩行

```text
a girl walking
masterpiece,amazing quality,best quality,detailed hair,detailed eyes
```

### 2.4 カメラ上昇・後退

```text
A single character standing still, full body visible, facing forward, neutral pose.
The camera starts at eye level, front view, centered composition.

The camera slowly moves upward and backward, smoothly rising above the character.
The viewpoint gradually changes to a high-angle shot, looking down diagonally from above.
The character remains perfectly still and unchanged.
No change in facial expression, clothing, or body proportions.

Smooth cinematic camera movement, slow camera crane up, no camera shake.
Consistent lighting, consistent background, stable framing.
masterpiece,amazing quality,best quality,detailed hair,detailed eyes
```

## 3. Negative prompt サンプル

### 3.1 ジャンプ

```text
walking, drifting,
camera pan, camera zoom, camera shake,
sudden rotation, fast spin,
body distortion, twisted neck, broken shoulders,
extra arms, extra hands, duplicated face,
background warping, flickering
multiple_views,
text,signature
bad hands,extra digit
worst quality, low quality,bad anatomy
```

### 3.2 回転

```text
walking, jumping, drifting,
camera pan, camera zoom, camera shake,
sudden rotation, fast spin,
body distortion, twisted neck, broken shoulders,
extra arms, extra hands, duplicated face,
background warping, flickering
multiple_views,
text,signature
bad hands,extra digit
worst quality, low quality,bad anatomy
```

### 3.3 歩行・カメラ上昇共通

```text
multiple_views,
text,signature
bad hands,extra digit
worst quality, low quality,bad anatomy
```

## 4. 利用時の注意

- これらは入力例であり、品質が検証済みの標準プロンプではない。
- 回転の negative prompt には、目的の動作と重なる `sudden rotation`、`fast spin` が含まれる。意図した動作を抑制する可能性があるため、標準化する前に出力比較が必要である。
